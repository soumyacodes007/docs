Choose your app's eligibility policy separately from private record storage. Arkognito handles
one-time ownership and optional Semaphore participation. Your application decides who may join
the group and verifies the evidence for that decision off-chain.

| Layer | Responsibility | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core SDK | Encryption, relaying, one-time ownership, recovery and record discovery | Implemented |
| Semaphore module | Anonymous group proofs, scoped nullifiers and independent verification | Implemented and tested; experimental |
| Admin-approved commitments | Your admin admits eligible users through `addSemaphoreMembers` | Supported group API; eligibility is your policy |
| Anon Aadhaar admission | Verify an Aadhaar credential proof, then admit its bound random commitment | Synthetic test proving only; combined adapter not implemented |
| ZK Email admission | Verify an application-specific email credential proof | Deferred; no complete proof/admission integration |

## The admission boundary

1. The user derives a random-secret group commitment in their own vault.
2. Your admission service verifies the chosen eligibility evidence.
3. It checks freshness, audience, issuer/key policy and binding to that target commitment/group.
4. Your admin enrolls the approved commitment.
5. Subsequent records use Semaphore membership proofs through the same SDK workflow.

<Warning>
  There is no production credential adapter or public "approve me" endpoint included. A Boolean
  supplied by a browser is not eligibility evidence. Preventing repeated enrollment is a separate
  policy; Semaphore limits identities already admitted to a group, not unique people.
</Warning>

Keep credentials, attester data and private admission records off Arkiv. The hackathon forbids
plaintext or hashed personal data, and this repository requires synthetic demonstrations.
Do not publish real credential proofs or credential-derived nullifiers. Use synthetic credentials
and synthetic records for demonstrations.

Start with [anonymous group permissions](/guides/anonymous-membership). See the
[Anon Aadhaar integration boundary](/integrations/anon-aadhaar) when your app needs that policy.
